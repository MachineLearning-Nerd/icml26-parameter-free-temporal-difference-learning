# Claim 1 limitations and deviations

The stochastic experiment is a broad finite family, not by itself a universal
proof. The proof replay supplies the universal algebra from the paper's stated
lemmas. It corrects and discloses one appendix index typo: an `i`-indexed product
prints `alpha^t`; the next line and valid unrolling require `alpha^i`.

The phrase “optimal” also relies on the standard stochastic estimation lower
bound cited by the paper; this campaign verifies the achieved bias/variance
form and its last-iterate behavior, not the paper's novelty priority claim.
